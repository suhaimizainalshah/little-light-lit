Turn any video into a lesson

Add questions, polls and notes at the right moments. The video pauses for each one, students answer, and you see who understood what. No student accounts, and answers stay with you and your students.

A video file makes a lesson that works offline. A YouTube lesson is a tiny file, but students need internet.

Share the lesson with students

Students hand in with a file, a code, email or your Google Sheet.

Scores and answers go straight into the platform’s gradebook, including on offline Moodle devices such as Beekee.

Embed it on a website

Put the student lesson file online (your school website, GitHub Pages or Netlify Drop all work), then paste its address here for embed code.
